# Reference / asset policy

- Google Maps, satellite and Street View may be used for human visual reference and location checking only.
- Do not copy, scrape, export or bake Google imagery into shipped textures or geometry.
- Use OSM/open government datasets for reproducible road/building geometry subject to their licenses.
- Use official/public photos for visual verification subject to copyright limits; do not redistribute photos as game textures without permission.
- Use owned, commissioned, licensed or CC0/compatible PBR materials, vehicles, people and props.
- Real-world trademarks/signage should be reviewed for the intended release context; production can substitute legally-cleared equivalents where required.
