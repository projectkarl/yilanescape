# v2.3 reference-lock notes

These notes are for art QA. They do not grant permission to copy third-party imagery as textures.

## Yilan Station / Jimmy Square
- Official Yilan tourism describes Jimmy Square / Diudiudang Forest as directly opposite Yilan Station.
- The official description identifies **nine steel trees approximately 14 m high** and the suspended green "flying train" artwork.
- The official old-city itinerary describes Yilan Station as having a Jimmy-themed painted exterior and a smiling giraffe landmark.
- Source: https://travel.yilan.gov.tw/zh-tw/attraction/232/
- Source: https://travel.yilan.gov.tw/zh-tw/tour/12/

The v2.3 `SM_Yilan_Station_Facade_Hero_HQ` is therefore a higher-detail **reference model**, not photogrammetry. Final production sign-off still requires manual photo matching for width, bay proportions, roofline, mural breakup, signage, giraffe proportions and surrounding curb/forecourt geometry.

## Yilan Distillery
- Heritage references show historic red-brick warehouse/factory architecture and visible industrial details.
- Source: https://nchdb.boch.gov.tw/city/ILN/assets/overview/historicalBuilding/20140128000001

## Google / Street View use
Google Maps / Street View can be used by artists as a visual cross-check where terms permit, but screenshots/tiles are not shipped as game textures. Geometry and production textures must come from permitted source data, original art, licensed assets or field capture with appropriate rights.
