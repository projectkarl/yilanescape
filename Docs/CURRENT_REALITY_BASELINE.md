# Current reality baseline — 2026-09-30

The production world should treat these as externally verified anchor facts:

- Jimmy Square / Diudiudang Forest is at Yixing Rd Sec. 1 and approximately 1–2 minutes on foot from Yilan Station; it includes a tall steel-tree canopy and suspended train artwork.
- Yilan Distillery is on Jiucheng W. Rd and is a major old-city anchor; its historic warehouse/brick character must be visible.
- Yilan Administration Memorial Hall is on Jiucheng S. Rd and uses Japanese-Western hybrid historic architecture with a garden setting.
- Current public transport engineering references include the intersections Zhongshan Rd–Xinmin Rd, Jiucheng E. Rd–Guangfu Rd, Nongquan Rd–Shennong Rd, and the Jiucheng S./Zhongshan/Shennong junction.
- National Freeway 5 runs through Yilan County with Toucheng and Yilan interchanges; the Xueshan Tunnel lies between Pinglin and Toucheng and is about 12.9 km long.

These anchors are not sufficient by themselves for 1:1 city completion. Every Tier A block still requires a dated map/street-view/photo comparison before sign-off.
